How We Work

A small, senior team means fewer handoffs and a straighter line from idea to working software

01 · Discovery

Understand the problem

We start by understanding what actually needs to change, including the workflow, the data, and the constraints, before proposing how to build it.

02 · Build

Ship in working slices

We build in small, working increments so progress is visible early, and direction can shift before too much is locked in.

03 · Iterate & Support

Stay maintainable

Software is handed over in a state someone else can maintain, with ongoing support available as things change.

Our technical approach

We work pragmatically across modern web stacks and cloud platforms, and reach for AI/LLM tooling only where it solves a concrete problem, not by default. We favor plain, well-understood solutions over unnecessary complexity, and we're upfront when something isn't a good fit for AI or automation at all.
